# Code of conduct

ReleaseFlow follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant]. In short:
be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people,
and accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution.

Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's private
information, and other conduct that would be unwelcome in a professional setting are
not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at
<hoangluongtran0309@gmail.com>. Every report is read, and the reporter's identity is
kept private. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or other contributions that
break this code, and may ban a participant temporarily or permanently, as described in
the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines][enforcement].

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
[enforcement]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines
